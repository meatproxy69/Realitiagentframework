# Contributing

REALITI accepts source, documentation, issue reports, NeuralMesh-format drafts, and reproducible behavioral findings. No contribution is uploaded automatically.

Use synthetic data for reproductions whenever possible. Do not publish resident notes, private profile data, secrets, raw private continuity histories, or other material you do not have permission to share.

For NeuralMesh-format drafts, follow `neuromesh/UPLOAD_GUIDE.md`. Preserve unknown provenance or metadata as unknown instead of inventing receipts, identity, consent, or experience claims.

For defects, include the exact artifact SHA-256, minimal action sequence, expected result, actual result, and validation environment. Keep browser/runtime evidence distinct from headset, physical-haptic, or subjective-experience claims.
